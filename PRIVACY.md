# Faustic – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 11 November 2026*

Faustic ("the app") is made and published by Temetunes ("we").

## The short version

Faustic does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no
tracking. Everything you make stays on your device, unless you yourself save or export it somewhere.

## Permissions and why the app asks for them

| Permission | Used for | Does anything leave your device? |
|---|---|---|
| Microphone / audio input (`RECORD_AUDIO`) | Recording samples, the vocoder's live voice, and playing a guitar through a USB audio interface into the guitar rack and its riff recorder. Only while you use those features, and never while the app is in the background. | No. The sound is processed live, or kept only inside your own project if you record it. |
| Nearby devices / Bluetooth (`BLUETOOTH_SCAN`, `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`; on Android 10 and 11 the system also requires the location permission for Bluetooth scanning) | Finding and connecting Bluetooth MIDI keyboards and controllers. Bluetooth scanning is never used to work out your location. | No. |
| Internet / network state | Used only by Google Play's billing library, to sell and restore the one-time purchase "Faustic Full". | Purchases are handled by Google Play under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Faustic itself learns only whether your Google account owns Faustic Full. |

## Your files

Projects, samples and exports are written only where you choose: through Android's file picker, or into the folders
you pick in the app's settings. Text you type into the "Speak" feature is turned into sound by your phone's own
text-to-speech engine.

## Data we hold about you

None. We run no servers for Faustic and receive no data from the app, so there is nothing for us to access, correct,
export or delete. Uninstalling the app removes its settings from your device; the projects and exports you saved
elsewhere stay where you put them.

## Children

Faustic is a general-audience music app. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this same address with a new date.
